Dataset Ideas


Best Option: Crop Disease Resistance / Agriculture Breeding

This is probably the cleanest.

User query:

“Do we have any tomato cultivars resistant to drought + powdery mildew that are ready for spring planting?”

Traversal becomes:

Desired traits
→ resistance genes
→ cultivars
→ greenhouse trial results
→ seed inventory
→ field readiness
→ projected yield

Example data:

cultivars.xlsx
resistance_genes.fasta
greenhouse_trials.csv
soil_reports/
seed_inventory.csv
field_status.csv
protocols/

Why this is good:

extremely easy to fake believable data
obvious business value
timelines matter
genetics still matters
less domain scrutiny
easy for judges to grasp

The answer feels very real:

“Cultivar DR-Tomato-14 has both PMR-3 and DRT-2 markers, passed greenhouse trial batch 2026-04, 2,400 seeds available, suitable for spring deployment in arid soil conditions.”

That sounds insanely believable.
And the provenance graph still works perfectly.

Second Best: Fermentation / Microbial Strain Selection

SUPER good for your background.

Query:

“Which yeast strains can produce high citrus ester output under low-temperature fermentation?”

Traversal:

Flavor profile
→ metabolite compounds
→ microbial strains
→ fermentation runs
→ bioreactor inventory
→ production schedule

Data:

strains.csv
fermentation_logs/
metabolite_assays.csv
sequencing/
bioreactor_schedule.csv

Why good:

very biotech-feeling
operational scheduling matters
easier than plant genetics
judges think “real science AI”
fits your automation/lab background well
